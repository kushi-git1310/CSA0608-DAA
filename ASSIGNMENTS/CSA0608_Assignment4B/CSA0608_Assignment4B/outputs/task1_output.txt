Meaning of the columns:
  i        : sub-problem 'lay out words i..n-1'
  cand j   : first word of the NEXT line (words i..j-1 form the current line)
  unused s : W - length of that candidate line
  s^2 / 0  : penalty of that line (0 if it is the final line)
  best[j]  : already-solved optimum of the smaller sub-problem j..n-1
  total    : s^2 + best[j]  -> the minimum over j becomes best[i]
