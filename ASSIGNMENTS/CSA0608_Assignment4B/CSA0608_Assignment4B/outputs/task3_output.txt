Cycle weight re-computed independently = -2 (< 0)  -> shortest paths are UNDEFINED, no distances returned.
Detection mechanism: after |V|-1 passes every shortest path (at most |V|-1 edges) is final.
If the |V|-th pass can still relax an edge, some path uses >= |V| edges, i.e. repeats a vertex,
and only a negative cycle makes repetition profitable. Walking pred[] |V| times lands inside it.
